# Federal AI Literacy Academy

**Inspired by the U.S. Department of Labor's Artificial Intelligence Literacy Framework**

A free, interactive, gamified learning platform covering the **5 Foundational Content Areas of AI Literacy** — built for Department of Labor staff and all federal employees.

## 🎯 What This Is

This is a single-page web application that teaches the DOL's AI Literacy Framework through five progressive modules, each with interactive content, flip cards, animated charts, and knowledge-check quizzes. No tech background needed.

## 📚 The 5 Modules

| # | Module | What You'll Learn |
|---|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Understand AI Principles** | How AI works — patterns, training, hallucinations, and human oversight |
| 2 | **Explore AI Uses** | Real-world AI applications at DOL and across the federal workforce |
| 3 | **Direct AI Effectively** | Prompting techniques, contextual framing, and iteration strategies |
| 4 | **Evaluate AI Outputs** | Fact-checking, spotting gaps, and applying human judgment |
| 5 | **Use AI Responsibly** | Data protection, policy compliance, risk management, and accountability |

## 🎮 Gamification Features

- **XP Points** — Earn up to 500 XP across all modules
- **Badges** — Collect 5 unique badges (one per module)
- **Streak Counter** — Track your consecutive quiz passes
- **Gated Progression** — Must pass each quiz (2/3 correct) to unlock the next module
- **Leaderboard** — See how you rank upon completion
- **Confetti Celebrations** — Perfect scores trigger confetti!

## 📄 Source Documents

All content is sourced exclusively from official U.S. Department of Labor documents:

- **DOL 2025 AI Compliance Plan** — Governance, security, and responsible AI policies

Every fact and claim includes a citation to its source document and page number.

## 🚀 How to Use

3. No build tools, servers, or dependencies required — it's a single self-contained HTML file
